# EMMA Roadmap FY27 SOW
## Science and Restoration Integration Component

**Draft for review | $100,000 in-house USACE labor | 12 months from funding and staff availability**

### Problem Statement

UMRR workshops identified 49 research questions and reach-specific priorities. EMMA will connect these priorities to existing science, HREPs and monitoring records so the program can use accumulated knowledge, identify promising learning opportunities and direct limited research and monitoring resources toward actionable next steps.

### Objectives

Advance UMRR's strategic direction for science–restoration integration by helping the program:

- **Target learning investments:** identify which priority questions existing evidence can address and where additional study offers value (Objective 3.2).
- **Improve restoration decisions:** connect scientific findings and project experience to HREP planning and design (Objective 3.3).
- **Coordinate and communicate learning:** preserve shared evidence and decisions, assign follow-through and explain program value (Objectives 3.1, 1.2 and 4.1).

### Tasks

1. **Focus the pilot (Months 1–2).** Confirm selected workshop priorities with program and science/restoration leads. Choose one theme and two HREP examples, using mussel habitat and Steamboat Island as the starting example.
2. **Connect priorities to evidence (Months 3–6).** Review 20 research/restoration artifacts. Link questions to studies, findings, HREP features and monitoring records using AI-assisted extraction and expert review. Retain citations, uncertainty and missing connections.
3. **Compare learning opportunities (Months 7–9).** Develop reports and reusable R tools building on EMMA, Restoration Features and USACE knowledge standards. Compare opportunities by reach priority, evidence gap, available data, study feasibility, project timing and anticipated effort.
4. **Prepare action and handoff (Months 10–12).** Conduct two staff reviews. Recommend existing-data analyses, study-design improvements or future monitoring, with proposed responsibilities, dependencies and costs. Document operating effort and phased expansion needs.

### Budget

**$100,000 total:** scoping **$15,000**; evidence synthesis/review **$30,000**; tools/reporting **$35,000**; evaluation/handoff **$20,000**. Includes in-house development, scientific review and coordination. Assumes document access and reviewer availability; excludes new field monitoring, software procurement and funded partner labor.

### Deliverables

- **Priority-to-evidence report:** interim briefing by Month 6; final report linking selected questions to existing science, HREPs and knowledge gaps by Month 12.
- **Reusable evidence package:** reviewed links covering 20 artifacts and two HREPs, documented methods, an information-model crosswalk and R reporting tools.
- **Learning-opportunity action plan:** staff-reviewed shortlist with recommended next steps, proposed responsibilities, costs and a phased investment roadmap by Month 12.

This pilot will help the program select and prepare learning investments. Later funding can implement recommended studies and expand coverage; a complete program-wide database is outside this increment.
