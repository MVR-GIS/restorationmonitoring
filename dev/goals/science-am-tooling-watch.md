# Science and adaptive-management data tooling: baseline watch register

Baseline review: 2026-11-03. Re-screened against the user's clarification: track **implemented data models and software** for science–restoration integration and durable adaptive-management knowledge, preferably open source, across a large and long-lived portfolio. General AM guidance, construction/project tracking, monitoring portals and analytical models do not qualify by themselves. A useful partial component can still be watched for its specific contribution.

## Conclusion and correction to the earlier review

The earlier [landscape review](existing-models-tools-landscape-review.md) gave too much weight to the existence of adaptive-management programs and project/monitoring portals. RECOVER's program function and DIVER's project/monitoring portal do not, by themselves, demonstrate a data system for the specific process sought here. **Do not delay model and reporting development solely to seek a whole-system replacement.** This bounded review found no verified, openly reusable, end-to-end system covering the desired UMRR lifecycle; that is a finding about the sources inspected, not proof none exists. Several real systems and models are worth learning from and watching.

The working direction is to develop the missing integration capability with EMMA and existing UMRR systems as design anchors, while examining proven components and keeping a lightweight watch. This is not approval of a database schema, technology stack, application rewrite, or a claim that every component must be built. The user describes EMMA as the likely system of record for practitioner judgments for design purposes.

## What qualifies as evidence

The desired chain spans research questions and hypotheses, studies and datasets, projects and restoration actions, observation and analysis, evidence-linked findings, assessments, decisions and revisions. It needs attribution, uncertainty, historical gaps, multi-entry navigation and long-term stewardship across projects and organizations. A candidate need not do all of this to be useful, but its **demonstrated slice** must be stated. In particular, a coded decision model or a performance dashboard does not by itself establish a durable register of practitioner judgments.

Evidence tiers used below: **implemented** = identified software or schema and documentation of behavior; **research/proposal** = published design without a verified reusable implementation; **unverified lead** = ownership, code or function cannot be confirmed. "Open source" requires a specific inspectable license covering the relevant component: publicly visible code, an open document, free access, or an article's license is insufficient. Unknown means not verified, not absent. No candidate was installed or tested; version and activity signals are snapshots, not guarantees of sustainability.

The [published system paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401743041_A_data_management_system_for_long-term_natural_resource_monitoring_and_management_projects_with_multiple_cooperators) describes monitoring, actions and responses to recommendations in a centralized system. | Strong **implementation and institutional precedent** for science, monitoring, management and feedback over repeated decisions, beyond construction tracking. Its optimization and ecological decision model are a narrower use case than a general evidence-led portfolio. Next: locate an authoritative schema, code or more recent implementation report; stop at historical exemplar if these remain unavailable. |
| **Open Research Knowledge Graph (ORKG)** — an implemented scholarly knowledge system. It is not demonstrated to be a restoration AM system. Next: inspect R API and a small published contribution example; no new platform commitment. |
| **SEPIO LinkML** — inspectable [Apache-2.0 information model repository](https://github.com/sepio-framework/sepio-linkml). | A candidate vocabulary/model for claims, evidence and provenance; compare how evidence is distinguished from an assertion and from source lineage. ## Practical near-term learning

**Completed 2026-11-03:** the time-limited [Bertom mapping](../architecture/bertom-tool-model-mapping.md) uses accessible model and software documentation to compare AMMonitor, Miradi, SEPIO and AMModels. It did not inspect the AMMonitor dictionary or a Miradi exchange file; those remain unverified, not necessary follow-up before proceeding with design.

1. Map a minimal **synthetic Bertom** example to AMMonitor's published dictionary when available, Miradi's exchange objects, and SEPIO's assertion/evidence model. Record source version, exact terms and relationships, useful fit, missing concepts and unresolved semantics. Preserve the real absence of an EMMA monitoring task; do not manufacture one.
2. Compare AMModels' published model/metadata organization and ORKG's contribution descriptions against one analysis and finding. Keep an expert-authored finding, assessment and decision distinct; describe disagreement and revision explicitly.
3. Use NPAM's documented monitoring/action/recommendation loop to test whether the proposed model represents actual management feedback, while keeping its historical and use-case boundaries clear.
4. Continue conceptual design and report prototypes in parallel. Escalate a potential replacement only if a named, usable tool demonstrates substantial coverage and plausible reuse terms; otherwise reuse specific patterns or components and retain the candidate on watch.
